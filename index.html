Worli Play – Market Timings, Results & Daily Quiz

Worli Play is an easy-to-use informational app that provides market opening and closing timings, published results, and educational quiz content in a clean and fast interface.

The app is designed to help users quickly access daily market schedules, published results, and quiz-based learning content in one place.

  • Market opening & closing timings
  • Published daily results
  • Daily educational quiz
  • Fast and simple interface
  • Regular content updates
